L-SECTION PDF CHECKER

Runs on this laptop. No GPU, no model, no internet, no cost.

Start it (in a terminal, from the Fine-Tuning folder):
    .venv\Scripts\python checker\app.py
then open  http://localhost:7861  in a browser. Ctrl+C in the terminal stops it.

Use it
1. Upload a Plan & L-Section PDF (or pick one of the PDFs in the Fine-Tuning folder) and click "Check the PDF".
   A whole drawing set takes a few seconds.
2. The summary and the findings table appear:
     FLAG   a value outside its requirement
            - bridge FL below MIN FL REQ.  ("FLAG: For bridge X min. FL = .., and FL = ..")
            - track distance below 4.725 m (IR Schedule of Dimensions, new works)
            - proposed gradient steeper than the ruling gradient (note 6)
     CHECK  values on the drawing that disagree
            - data-band arithmetic: FL - GL vs cut/fill, RL - FL vs note 5 (762 mm), FL - existing FL vs difference row
            - free board printed vs FL - HFL
            - gradient labels vs each other and vs the FLs of the grade points
            - plan vs L-section curve boxes; transition points vs curve box (TRL / CCL); Shift and Degree formulas
            - plan vs L-section bridge callouts
     INFO   incomplete or worth a look (missing proposal heights, blank callouts, blank band columns,
            TTL differing from the simple formula on large-angle curves)
3. Click a row to see the finding on its sheet (numbered box) and close up. Pick any sheet from the "Sheet" list.
4. "Download the findings (CSV)" opens in Excel.
5. Ask questions in the box at the bottom, e.g.
     bridge 560            ground level at bridge 532       ROB-239       LC 226
     CH 1242662.9          1240+766.8                       curve 377
     which bridges are flagged        list all problems     summary        TBM BMT38
   Answers come straight from the PDF's values (nearest data-band column, no interpolation) and cite the
   sheet and chainage.

Limits
- PDFs only, and only vector PDFs (text can be selected/searched). Scanned sheets are not supported yet.
- The layout must be the Plan & L-Section layout of these drawings; other pages are reported as
  "Layout not recognised" instead of being checked.
